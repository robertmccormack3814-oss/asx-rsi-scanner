Simulation rules. The account begins with A$5,000 on 15 Aug 2026. Each accepted trade uses a fixed A$500 cash slot: A$5 is charged immediately as buy commission and A$495 buys the stock at the scanner's recorded entry price. At the recorded strategy exit, the shares are sold at the scanner's exit price and another A$5 commission is deducted. Exit proceeds are then available for later signals. A maximum of 10 positions can be open at once. Signals before the simulation start date are ignored. Where several events share the same scanner date, exits are treated as occurring before new entries so released capital can be reused on that scan. This is a bookkeeping simulation only; it does not model bid/ask spread, slippage, partial fills, taxes, dividends, market impact or whether the quoted price was actually executable.
